🇺🇸 English Section (English Testing)

Rhasspy Piper is a fast, local neural text-to-speech system designed to run on resource-constrained devices with high naturalness. It uses the VITS architecture to synthesize speech directly from phonemes in real time.

By implementing an offscreen document in Chrome Manifest V3, this extension provides low latency audio playback and instantaneous seek controls. Try moving your cursor over this second paragraph to experience the hover-to-seek feature.
